About Us — Al Safa Exhibition Stands Manufacturing LLC

Our mission is to serve our clients with dynamic and integrated point of sale material solutions for their end-to-end marketing needs through below the line advertising, retail branding, merchandising, and printing solutions. We offer expertise in conceptual development, strategy, design and production of point-of-purchase branding and tools that simplify retail branding and marketing and provide full spectrum of visual appeal.​

Our company is ISO certified for quality, health, and safety, and environment. We are SEDEX certified and members of the exchange. We manufacture a variety of point of sale materials for the UAE market. By utilizing our expertise and experience in the point of purchase market, we will make sure you receive the best value for your money.

Our work encompasses a wide range of areas, including:

  • Visual Merchandising, Retail Graphics & Branding
  • Advertising Displays & Point-of-Sale Materials (POSM)
  • Retail Fit-out, Shopfitting & Fixtures, Shop Furniture
  • Window Branding, Signage & Display Counter
  • Corporate Events, Exhibitions & Promotional Projects
